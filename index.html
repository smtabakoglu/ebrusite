About Me

Ebru Tabakoglu has been providing individual counselling sessions for many years. Her work primarily focuses on adult consultees experiencing mild anxiety and individuals affected by significant life events such as marriage, divorce, parenthood, career changes, and financial difficulties.

As the holder of a Master of Arts Degree in Applied Psychology, Mrs. Tabakoglu is professionally qualified to support individuals in these areas. In addition, her Master of Arts Degree in Educational Sciences and numerous professional certifications provide a strong academic and professional foundation for her work

Ebru completed a three-year Gestalt Therapy Training under the supervision of Assistant Professor Hanna Nita Scherler, a respected Clinical Psychology expert with a PhD from Fielding Graduate University, Santa Barbara, USA.

She also worked with Dr. Emel Stroup in the areas of Cognitive Therapy Inclination Interview and Therapy Skills, strengthening her communication and analytical approach in understanding the root causes of clients’ challenges.
