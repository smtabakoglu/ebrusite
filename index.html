About Me

Ebru Tabakoglu has been providing individual counselling sessions for many years. Her work primarily focuses on adult consultees experiencing mild anxiety and individuals affected by significant life events such as marriage, divorce, parenthood, career changes, and financial difficulties.

As the holder of a Master of Arts Degree in Applied Psychology, Mrs. Tabakoglu is professionally qualified to support individuals in these areas. In addition, her Master of Arts Degree in Educational Sciences and numerous professional certifications provide a strong academic and professional foundation for her work

Ebru completed a three-year Gestalt Therapy Training under the supervision of Assistant Professor Hanna Nita Scherler, a respected Clinical Psychology expert with a PhD from Fielding Graduate University, Santa Barbara, USA.

She also worked with Dr. Emel Stroup in the areas of Cognitive Therapy Inclination Interview and Therapy Skills, strengthening her communication and analytical approach in understanding the root causes of clients’ challenges.

Ebru received NLP and Milton Model Hypnosis Techniques Training directly from Richard Bandler, founder of Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP), in London. This training qualified her both as an NLP practitioner and trainer at international level.

In addition, Ebru completed a two-year Individual Psycho-Drama Training at Zerka Moreno Institute in Istanbul and received Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT) Training from Gary Craig, enabling her to provide EFT and kinesiology-based approaches.

Ebru also obtained the Herrmann Holistic Thinking Model Training Certificate in London through participation in a specialised Herrmann Leadership Camp. This training supports her coaching work in areas such as career development, stress management, leadership, and problem-solving.

Furthermore, she completed Professional Erickson Coaching Training in Canada, taking an important step toward becoming an International Professional Coach with ACTP Certification.

Most recently, Ebru completed the Transactional Analysis TA101 course through Berne Institute in the United Kingdom and continues both foundation and advanced level studies under the facilitation of Adrienne Lee.
